Working privacy note

Privacy and discretion.

Last updated September 1, 2026

What this covers

This note explains how Tandem intends to handle information submitted through the no-signup Ambition Archetype Quiz, private founding NYC community form, partner inquiry form, and related early pilot conversations. It is a working note for the pilot stage and should become a fuller privacy policy before broader public launch.

What the quiz collects

The archetype quiz does not ask for your name, email, gender, dating preferences, age, location, or social profiles. It may temporarily remember quiz progress on your device so you can return to your result. Tandem stores only the primary and secondary archetype if you choose to email yourself the result or carry that result into the founding community form.

If you email yourself a result

After the quiz result appears, you can optionally enter an email address and first name so Tandem can send you that result. This is separate from joining the founding list. Future Tandem updates should only be sent if you choose the optional updates checkbox.

What the founding form may collect

The founding community form may ask for basic contact information, age range, NYC location context, gender identity, who you are interested in meeting, relationship intention, event interest, general availability, how you heard about Tandem, optional social links, and optional free-text context.

How responses are used

Responses are used to understand interest, review potential founding cohorts, plan small curated NYC experiences, send Tandem invitations or updates when appropriate, and improve the early concept. Tandem may also look at aggregated or anonymized patterns, such as general availability or event interest.

What is not public

Tandem should not publicly display private dating preferences, compatibility answers, contact details, social links, or partner inquiry details. If a community or venue helps co-host an edition, participant-level form responses should not be shared with that partner without consent.

Partner inquiries

Partner inquiry responses are stored separately from participant applications and are used to review potential community, membership organization, venue, or event collaborations.

Your choices

Participation is optional. Future-event or marketing consent should be collected clearly in the form where needed. You can ask Tandem to stop contacting you or request deletion of your submitted information by emailing hello@tandem-dating.com.
