Working privacy note

Privacy and discretion.

Last updated September 1, 2026

What this covers

This note explains how Tandem intends to handle information submitted through the founding NYC community interest form and related early pilot conversations. It is a working note for the pilot stage and should become a fuller privacy policy before broader public launch.

What we may collect

The form may ask for basic contact information, NYC location context, dating intention, availability, optional social links, and compatibility signals such as lifestyle rhythm, ambition style, or what matters in a partner.

How responses are used

Responses are used to understand interest, review potential founding cohorts, plan small curated NYC experiences, and send future Tandem invitations or follow-ups when appropriate.

What is not public

Tandem should not publicly display private dating preferences, compatibility answers, or contact details. If a partner community or venue helps co-host an edition, private form responses should not be shared with that partner without consent.

Your choices

Participation is optional. Future-event or marketing consent should be collected clearly in the form where needed, and anyone should be able to ask Tandem to stop contacting them.
